SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VisionGuard difficult to set up?
Not at all! VisionGuard is designed to be plug-and-play. Connect the hardware, download the app, scan the QR code, and you're protected in under 15 minutes. No programming knowledge required.
Will this damage my Smart TV?
Absolutely not. VisionGuard is a non-invasive external security layer. It monitors TV network traffic and sends IR signals just like any normal remote control. Your TV remains completely safe and under full warranty.
Does it work without internet?
Yes! The IR remote control and local sensor automation work completely offline. Internet is only required for push notifications and remote access from outside your home.
Which TV and AC brands are supported?
VisionGuard supports 15+ major brands including Samsung, LG, Sony, Hisense, Carrier, Mitsubishi, Panasonic, and more. The custom IR learning mode means it can learn any infrared remote you own.
Is my data private and secure?
All data is encrypted and stored in your personal Firebase account. VisionGuard does not sell or share your data. OTP authentication ensures only you have access to your system.
